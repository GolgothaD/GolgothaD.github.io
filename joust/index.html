❓ Rules & Scoring

How a joust works, and what everything's worth.

How a joust works

  • Higher lance wins. If your bird is even a little above the enemy when you collide, they pop into an egg. If they're above you — you're done.
  • Grab the eggs. Eggs bounce, land, and hatch into tougher riders if you leave them. Scoop them fast for bonus points.
  • Use the ledges. Land to run, skid, and line up your height. You wrap around the sides of the arena.
  • Mind the lava. The bottom ledge is the only safe footing down low. Fall in and you lose a life.

Who you're up against

Bounder — 500 pts. Slow, predictable. Wave 1 and up.
Hunter — 750 pts. Chases your altitude. Wave 2 and up.
Shadow Lord — 1,000 pts. Fast and relentless. Wave 4 and up.
Egg — 250–500 pts. Worth more the faster you grab it.

Clear every rider in a wave to advance. Every 5th wave is an Egg Wave — free eggs, free points, no excuses.

Controls: D-pad ◀ ▶ steer, ▲ flap, ▼ dive — A flaps, B pauses. On desktop: arrow keys steer, Space flaps, P pauses. A pocket tribute to the 1982 arcade classic.
