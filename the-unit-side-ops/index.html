    Fan-made, inspired-by tribute. Mission stages echo episodes of The Unit (Season 1's plane-rescue premiere “First Responders”, the Spain exfil in “Non-Permissive Environment”, and a night compound raid in the spirit of “The Kill Zone”) — retold with original characters: team leader Jonah “Doc” Reyes, new man Bobby Kane, and on the Home Front, Mara Reyes, newcomer Kate Kane, and Tess Ward. Progress lives only for this session — close the tab and the tour restarts, just like a deployment.
